Welcome to twisted wonderland!

about

Twisted wonderland is a disney villian inspired graphic novel combined fighting game, produced by disney itself! Twisted wonderland follows the story of a student yuu, who gets isekaid into the twisted wonderland universe. Yuu gets transported into the magic private school NRC where they reside as a student until they find their way back home. Throughout the storyline we are introduced to the 7 different dorms, where you experience a different a storyline about each dorm.
